# Published CAD and FEM collection: 100 cases

The current web-sourced collection is recorded in `published-100.json`. It contains 100 distinct geometry-file hashes paired with existing FEM results across six sources. No geometry, tasks or reference FEM solves were generated locally.

Artifacts:
- `cvpr2027/output/pdf/published-cad-fem-100.pdf`: 103 pages, one case per page plus scope/source documentation.
- `cvpr2027/output/datasets/published-cad-fem-100.zip`: original geometry/results files, published illustrations, available solver inputs, a portable manifest, numerical references and validation.
- `cvpr2027/output/datasets/published-cad-fem-100/manifest.json`: local inspection manifest.

## Limits

This is a sourced collection spanning several analyses, not every mechanical engineering domain or 100 uniformly complex industrial assemblies. It includes simpler parts, cantilever plates and standard benchmarks alongside intricate lattices and nonlinear shells/columns. Only four cases have verified dimensioned geometry illustrations; other models/views do not replace such drawings.

SFEM CAD was generated by BrepGen by the source authors, not here. Its exported fields are component-wise stochastic medians without realized load coordinates; they cannot serve as fully specified deterministic solver-reproduction cases. One inconsistent all-zero displacement case was excluded. Reference fields were verified byte-for-byte against the original publisher tar archive; STEP contents match publisher Git blob identifiers.

Bath parameter sweep rows with zero placeholders must not be treated as solved FEM results. The Linear case was excluded because its FE native CAD is missing. Wing Test4 was excluded for its filename/result fraction discrepancy. Meta-shell thickness cases were excluded for their CAD 31-degree versus results 30-degree discrepancy. Fracture-toughness specimens remain candidates until their numerical model correspondence is established. COMSOL-dependent legacy cases are separate and excluded from this collection.

Results can be read without COMSOL. Re-running some analyses may require commercial solvers. Model trials and model scores are absent. CAD creation/edit plus FEM and FEM with supplied CAD must remain separate eventual evaluation tracks; missing drawing or loading specifications are not invented to qualify cases.
