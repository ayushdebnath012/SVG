# Assembly (follows the article sequence)

1. **Ring gear.** Press 16 Ø6 pins into the housing base. The article made
   them hollow and slipped 3 mm steel rod inside for strength; best is a
   solid Ø6.00 ground rod 35 mm long. Stack per pin:
   `7 mm spacer / 686 bearing / 3 mm spacer / 686 bearing / 7 mm spacer`.
2. **Dry mesh test.** Drop one cycloidal disk in. Push it sideways while
   turning — it should walk backwards around the ring. Repeat with both
   disks 180° apart (match the witness marks).
3. **Input shaft.** Assemble the 4-section eccentric shaft, one 6802-class
   bearing per cam plus spacers. Sections clamp with 2 × M3 through-bolts
   (holes printed slightly undersize so they self-tap). Thread the shaft
   through both disks as you stack it — you cannot insert the disks after.
4. **Output shaft.** Build 6 output pin stacks
   (printed Ø6 pin + 2 × 686 + spacers on M3×20) on the output flange,
   feed them through the Ø16 holes in the disks with the disks opposed
   180°, then close with the second flange (heat-set M3 inserts first).
5. **Main bearings.** Seat the 2 × 6807 (35×47×7) in housing base + lid.
   Drop the input+disks+output cartridge into the housing.
6. **Lid.** Press the lid over all 16 ring pins (tight — even pressure),
   add M4 inserts in the housing back, close with M4×40.
7. **Motor.** Bolt heat-set inserts into the input-shaft tail, attach the
   printed coupler, mount the NEMA 17 bracket to the housing, couple it up.
   Add M3/M4 inserts to the output face for your load horn/wheel.

Spin by hand first: input and output must counter-rotate smoothly at 15:1.
