Finite element model for cemented total hip replacements: investigation of cement fixation

Citation

Hua, Xijin and Jin, Zhongmin (2015) Finite element model for cemented total hip replacements: investigation of cement fixation. University of Leeds. [Dataset] https://doi.org/10.5518/13

Dataset description

The dataset was created as supplementary materials for the paper "The effect of cup outer sizes on the contact mechanics and cement fixation of cemented total hip replacements". The dataset included the following documentation and data: the predicted results from the FE model; the FE models for the total hip replacements; the solid models of the components for the FE models;
