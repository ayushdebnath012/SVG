\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Structured 3D generation and editing.}
DeepCAD~\cite{wu2021deepcad} models parametric construction histories, and Text2CAD~\cite{khan2024text2cad} generates sequential CAD from text. CAD-Editor~\cite{cadeditor2025} directly studies instruction-based sequence editing. BenchCAD~\cite{benchcad2026} releases programmatic mechanical tasks and edit pairs; neuralCAD-Edit~\cite{neuralcadedit2026} evaluates expert-requested multimodal edits with geometry and feature-oriented measures. Our study uses released supervision and a standard code model. It does not reproduce these systems or establish superiority over them. It instead reports what a modest local adaptation achieves and where execution, bulk geometry and projected edit measurements disagree.

\paragraph{Vector views and visual representations.}
Drawing2CAD~\cite{drawing2cad2025} connects vector drawings to CAD histories. DeepSVG~\cite{carlier2020deepsvg} and StarVector~\cite{rodriguez2025starvector} learn vector graphics representations and generation; SVGEditBench~\cite{svgeditbench} evaluates instruction-based SVG edits. Our projections are deterministically derived from executed CAD. This preserves shared geometric provenance across views, but does not evaluate image understanding, SVG-to-CAD inference or annotation recovery. The added silhouette/change-mask audit is a diagnostic of visual shape changes, not a learned multi-view consistency objective.

\paragraph{Physical evaluation.}
FEM-Bench~\cite{fem_bench} evaluates scientific code generation, while CADEngBench~\cite{cadengbench2026} studies functional CAD editing and matched linear-static analysis. We use declared diagnostic loads and materials for a small mechanical subset; these properties are not supplied by the public edit pairs. The resulting FEM checks are external post-edit evaluations. No public runnable CADEngBench release was verified in this work, and its results are not presented as our reproduced benchmark.
