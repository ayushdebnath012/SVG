Why Design Lab exists

Looking right isn’t being right

AI can generate CAD that renders beautifully but can’t be machined, assembled, or changed. Today’s benchmarks reward the shape. Industry asks:

Can it be produced? Can an engineer keep working on it?

Design Lab measures exactly that — on real-world CAD data, scored by professional CAD engineers.

How we score

Automated floor, expert ceiling

Automated checks set the floor.

  • File validity
  • Geometry & topology
  • Constraints & interference
  • Edit locality

Expert judgment sets the ceiling.

  • Design intent
  • Manufacturability
  • Assemblability
  • Editability

Scored blind. Scored pairwise. Scored by people who ship real parts.
