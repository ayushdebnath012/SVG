Built on Real Engineering Standards

BenchCAD parts aren't synthetic primitives. About half the families — 52 of 106 — are anchored to real specification tables drawn from 47 ISO · DIN · EN · ASME · IEC codes, so a correct reconstruction is a spec-faithful, manufacturable part rather than a merely similar-looking shape. The rest follow common engineering practice.
