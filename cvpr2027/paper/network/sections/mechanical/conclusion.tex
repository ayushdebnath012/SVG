\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This is a small, single-seed code-conditioned CAD editing study. The online supervision comes from a synthetic mechanical benchmark re-partitioned locally; family/template checks do not prove complete geometric-ancestry isolation. A documented instruction/target conflict and one invalid STEP reference show that published labels require further auditing. Context filtering removes some of the longest parts and edit patches. Predicted code is limited by the executor and its time budget, and the current comparison checks solid volume agreement rather than feature-specific engineering tolerances. Our SVG views are deterministic projections; no learned drawing perception, annotation binding or production-sheet reconstruction is evaluated.

The structural controls are deterministic and use bounded grammars. Their equilibrium and clearance results cannot be transferred to arbitrary CAD shapes or interpreted as general safety certification. The full mechanical benchmark has no author-specified physical contract. We add a separate post-hoc 3D FEM audit of six illustrated edits under documented synthetic fixtures and loads; its response checks do not establish operating safety.

The completed workflow links published CAD edit supervision, validated program patches, executed solids and vector views. By reporting schema adherence, target syntax, actual solid agreement and operational failures separately, it makes the supported outcome reviewable. Stronger evidence requires larger contexts, multiple seeds and models, feature-preservation metrics, genuine drawing-conditioned editing and physical tasks with independently specified analysis contracts.
