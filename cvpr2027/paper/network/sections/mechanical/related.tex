\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
DeepCAD~\cite{wu2021deepcad} studies parametric construction histories, Text2CAD~\cite{khan2024text2cad} generates CAD from text, and CAD-Editor~\cite{cadeditor2025} studies instruction-based sequence editing. BenchCAD~\cite{benchcad2026} releases programmatic mechanical tasks and edit pairs. We use public task and reference artifacts with a disclosed local split; results are not an official leaderboard submission or a reproduction of these systems.

Graph of Thoughts~\cite{besta2023graphthoughts} motivates exploration of multiple candidate states, while UCT~\cite{kocsis2006uct} balances value estimates and exploration in a tree. Our candidate values are imperfect verification proxies; classical search guarantees are not assumed for language-model proposals or these proxies.

FEM-Bench~\cite{fem_bench} and CADEngBench~\cite{cadengbench2026} connect generated engineering artifacts with numerical evaluation. Our finite-element checks operate under specified synthetic material, load and fixture contracts. They assess a declared scenario and do not recover the intended physical use of a public CAD edit task.
