\section{Public data and local protocol}
\label{sec:data}
\paragraph{Two native representations.}
The retained public-data split contains 2,540 training, 172 validation and 252 test tasks (Table~\ref{tab:data-primary}). BenchCAD provides CadQuery source/target programs, natural-language edits and released STEP references~\cite{benchcad2026}. Microsoft's CAD-Editor provides native six-bit sketch--extrude edit sequences~\cite{cadeditor2025}. We retain their different representations and backends rather than treating them as interchangeable histories. CAD-Editor reference solids are reconstructed locally through our decoder; they are not author-released STEP references.

\paragraph{Provenance and isolation.}
BenchCAD is pinned at revision \texttt{5919f578ab09ec283603a082fab07c7639ab56eb} and declares CC-BY-4.0. Its 748 edit pairs yield 710 after family-based exclusion of pipe/electrical content. Family groups are joined when source/target AST templates match after numeric-literal masking; whole connected components are assigned to local splits before patch construction. The earlier retained test slice had 126 tasks; two additional pipe-related instructions are excluded from the primary experiment, yielding 124. CAD-Editor uses a documented subset of the public training/evaluation archive. Exact source/target connected components are isolated within each source; cross-source geometric ancestry is not established.

\paragraph{Representation and retention.}
The saved local split and its omissions are held fixed for the Astra comparisons. Comments and blank lines are normalized when preparing CadQuery patches. Original instructions are retained without adding target-line hints. Native command sequences use a separate schema and geometry decoder. The training partition supplies retrieval examples only; no weights are fitted by the active pipeline.

\paragraph{Evaluation boundaries.}
BenchCAD's edit release was originally held out; locally partitioning it into retrieval and evaluation subsets changes the benchmark contract. We report local held-out agreement, not official leaderboard or official-test generalization. The primary denominator includes all 252 retained tasks, including unavailable comparisons. Reference availability is tracked separately. A previously identified instruction/target conflict in a shaft-slot record illustrates that reference agreement does not certify natural-language compliance for every label. The graph-search analysis uses the 124 BenchCAD tasks, including one invalid reference in its overall denominator.
