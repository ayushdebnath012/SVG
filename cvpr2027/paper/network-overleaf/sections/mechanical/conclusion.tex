\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\ours implements CAD editing as proposal, execution, diagnosis and patch repair around frozen Astra. The recorded graph pipeline reaches \pipeFull/\pipeN{} strict matches against \pipeAstra/\pipeN{} for the original answer. Retrieval alone reaches \pipeRag/\pipeN{}, but its gain is post hoc and nonsignificant. These results support investigation of verifier quality rather than a claim of improved general editing performance.

The local split is not an official benchmark protocol. One recorded search run, unequal inference budgets, proxy constraints, a view-based semantic judge and scenario-specific FEM limit conclusions. Executable geometry may still implement the wrong feature; valid physical response under a synthetic load does not establish the intended use. Future comparisons should fix candidate-generation and verification budgets, use independently defined feature checks and report multiple seeds. The implementation supports broader decomposition and memory-update stages, but unexercised capabilities are not attributed to the reported experiment.
